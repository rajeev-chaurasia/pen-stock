That combined figure is an artifact of how this test splits its
traffic, not a property of the cache. A hit rate only means
something next to the request mix that produced it, and the mix
here was chosen to measure both sides, not to look good.

What this does and does not show
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A hit costs a hash and a map lookup and never leaves the process,
so the ratio above is mostly a statement about how slow the
simulated upstream is. Against a faster upstream the ratio shrinks;
against a real provider on a bad day it grows. The transferable
number is the absolute hit latency, not the speedup.
