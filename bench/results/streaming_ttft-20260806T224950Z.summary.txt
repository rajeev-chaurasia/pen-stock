Reading this
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The absolute TTFT figures are a property of the profile llmsim is
replaying. They are NOT any provider's real time to first token,
and quoting them as such would be a lie about a service that was
never called. The delta column is the part that is about Penstock.

The mean delta subtracts exactly. The per quantile deltas are
differences of quantiles and do not describe any single request:
see the same note in gateway_overhead.js.

At TIME_SCALE=1 the simulated TTFT is hundreds of milliseconds and
the gateway's contribution sits near the timer noise floor. Rerun
with a small TIME_SCALE to resolve it; the absolute numbers then
stop being profile realistic, which is the trade being made.
